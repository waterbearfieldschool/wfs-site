The busy season is winding down at Hannan Healthy Foods in Lincoln, so this
afternoon we helped turn one of the hoop houses over from summer to winter. The
tomatoes had finished, and the whole house was full of tall, tangled vines.

We pulled the plants, clipped them free of their strings and stakes, and filled
wagon after wagon to haul out to the compost pile. Pulling a loaded wagon across
the field turns out to be excellent work for a group of kids, and so is riding
back in an empty one.

The next day we finished the job: raking out the bed, loosening the soil with a
broadfork, and shaping a fresh bed. That's where lettuce will grow under plastic
all winter. The seedlings are ready and waiting, and they go in this week.

Next up at the farm: planting the lettuce, building cold frames, hooking up
solar panels, rainwater capture and filtration, mapping the farm, and making
zines about what we find. If you'd like to join us, keep an eye on the
schedule.
