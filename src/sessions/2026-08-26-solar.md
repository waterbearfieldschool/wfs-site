We had good fun hooking up small solar panels to LEDs, motors with wheels, and some
simple voltmeters. Some folks made little improvised ‘solar cars’ out of cardboard,
and a bit of a race ensued on the basketball court. We also hooked up larger solar
panels to a ‘nibbler’ — a sheet-cutting attachment for a 12V drill — which let us cut
nice shapes out of cardboard. Looking forward to learning more about solar charging
at the next one.
