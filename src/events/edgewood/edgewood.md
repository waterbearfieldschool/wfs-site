In this youth STEM workshop at Edgeood Sailing School in Cranston, RI, We had a wonderful time at the Edgewood Sailing School in Cranston, RI, We explored salinity measurements using basic electronics on a breadboard. 

We used a simple oscillator chip (the '555') whose output frequency is a function of a resistance value in the circuit.  The 'resistor' in the circuit was our water sample (high salinity --> low resistance), and we hooked the output oscillation to a small speaker.  Result:  higher salinity meant higher audio pitch!
