We had a great time running a DIY local radio telemetry workshop at the Edgewood Sailing School in Cranston, RI.  The basic idea was to set up 'tilt' sensors on devices that would be placed on the sailboats;  the sensors would relay their data to a laptop on the dock, which would display the current degree of tilt and GPS location of all of the youth boats currently out in the bay.  

We were able to demonstrate basic functionality -- but real-life testing on the water helped us to figure out that our radio power settings were too low to achieve much range.  We're excited to try the same experiment again next summer with an improved design!
