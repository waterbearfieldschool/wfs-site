A Day at Waterbear

Tuesdays, Wednesdays & Thursdays · 9 AM – 2 PM

Each day has the same rhythm: hands-and-minds craft in the morning, real-world building for our farm neighbors in the afternoon.

Morning · 9 AM – 12

Craft & Making

We dive into a hands-on craft — learning the tools, materials, and skills of a maker. Kids and adults work side by side, picking up real technique through real projects.

🪚 Woodworking💧 Water🧶 Fiber Arts 🗿 Sculpture & Fabrication🌱 Plants & Animals 🎵 Music & Instrument Making🎨 Painting & Drawing

🥪 Midday — lunch together outside

Afternoon · ~12:30 – 2 PM

Bricolage — Build & Repair

We put the morning's skills to work on real projects for neighboring farms — a repair, a build, a problem to solve. Cold frames, benches, fences, simple solar, whatever the season and the farm need.

It's where making meets usefulness: the community outfits itself, and everyone leaves having built something that matters to someone.
