Craft Days

Tuesdays, Wednesdays & Thursdays · 9 AM – 2 PM

Every day is a different mix of skills and topics — kids and adults making things side by side, then putting those skills to work on real projects for our farm neighbors. Here's the range of what we get into:

🪚 Woodworking

Saws, drills, joinery — build furniture, cold frames, and structures.

💧 Water

Filters, rain catchment, and flow — clean water and how it moves.

🧶 Fiber Arts

Weaving, rope-making, and sewing — fiber into cordage and cloth.

🛠️ Sculpture & Fabrication

Clay, carving, molds, and making — shaping and fabricating by hand.

🌿 Plants & Animals

Plant ID, gardening, foraging, and the farm's animals.

🎵 Music & Instrument Making

Build simple instruments, then play together.

🎨 Painting & Drawing

Drawing and painting from life.

🔧 Build & Repair

We take on real Build & Repair projects for neighboring farms — fixing and making useful things, together.
