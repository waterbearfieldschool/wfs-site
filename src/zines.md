## How it works

Each title comes two ways:

- **Free PDF** — download, read, and print it yourself. Copy it, teach from it, remix it (released **CC BY‑SA**).
- **Hand-bound print edition** — a bench-friendly booklet we make on our own little press.

Every zine doubles as the companion to a Field Day workshop, and follows the same shape: **new, plain-language science up front**, and a **reprint from the archive** in back — a beautifully-illustrated public-domain bulletin or manual on the same topic, rediscovered and paired with what we now know.

## The Foundations series

One booklet (or a few) for each of the things a resilient place needs to make for itself:

**Air · Communication · Energy · Fabrication · Food · Heating &amp; Cooling · Shelter · Transport · Water**
