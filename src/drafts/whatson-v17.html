🎨 Craft Days

Every day is a different mix of skills and topics. Register for individual days on our Summer Schedule, or a weekday block on our Fall Schedule.

🪚 Woodworking
Saws, drills, joinery — build furniture, cold frames, and structures.
💧 Working with Water
Filters, rain catchment, and flow — clean water and how it moves.
🧶 Fiber Arts
Weaving, rope-making, and sewing — fiber into cordage and cloth.
🛠️ Sculpture & Fabrication
Clay, carving, molds, and making — shaping and fabricating by hand.
🌿 Plants & Animals
Plant ID, gardening, foraging, and the farm’s animals.
🎵 Music & Instrument Making
Build simple instruments, then play together.
🎨 Painting & Drawing
Drawing and painting from life.
🔧 Build & Repair
We take on real Build & Repair projects for neighboring farms — fixing and making useful things, together.

ℹ️ About

Hands-on, screen-free learning and building for kids and adults, on farms in the Lincoln, MA area. Ways to take part:

🎨 Craft DaysHands-on skills and topics — a different mix each day. 🔧 WorkshopsSingle-session deep-dives. 🛠️ ProjectsLong-running builds and experiments. 🌲 MeetupsCasual, drop-in gatherings — hikes, plant walks, and goats at the farm.

Waterbear Field School operates as a recreational and enrichment program, not a licensed school or daycare.
