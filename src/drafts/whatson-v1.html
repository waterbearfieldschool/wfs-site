What's onJournalProjectsGet in touch

Hands-on, screen-free workshops and projects for kids and their grown-ups — energy, water, food, shelter, climate, communication, fabrication, transport, arts & crafts. The day is analog (any electronics stay off to the side), and we meet on farms around Lincoln, with the meet-up spot sent to enrolled families each day. Grown-ups welcome to stay or drop off — a recreational program, not a licensed school or daycare.
