🎨 Craft Days

Register for individual days on our Summer Schedule, or for a ‘day block’ (e.g. ‘Tuesdays, Sept 7 – Oct 9’) on our Fall Schedule.

🪚 Wood & Water
Building with wood and working with water — furniture and structures, plus filters, rain catchment, and flow.
🧶 Fiber & Paper
Weaving, rope-making, sewing, and papermaking — fiber into cordage, cloth, and paper.
🎨 Sculpture & Fabrication
Clay, carving, molds, and making — shaping and fabricating objects by hand.
🌿 Plants & Maps
Plant ID, gardening, and foraging — plus making our own maps and finding the way.
🎵 Music & Electricity
Make and play simple instruments, and tinker with circuits, motors, and off-grid power.

About

Hands-on, screen-free learning and building for kids and adults, on farms in the Lincoln, MA area. Four ways to take part:

🗓️ ProgramsSign up for a weekly meetup focused on a particular topic. 🔧 WorkshopsSingle-session deep-dives. 🛠️ ProjectsLong-running builds and experiments. 🌲 MeetupsCasual, drop-in gatherings — hikes, plant walks, and goats at the farm.

Waterbear Field School operates as a recreational and enrichment program, not a licensed school or daycare.
