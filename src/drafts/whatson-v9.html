🎨 Rotating Craft Schedule

Register for individual days on our Summer Schedule, or for a ‘day block’ (e.g. ‘Tuesdays, Sept 7 – Oct 17’) on our Fall Schedule. Craft days run 9 AM – 3 PM.

🪚 Woodworking
Build the shop's own furniture — sawhorse, bench, stool, toolbox.
🧶 Fiber Arts
Weaving, rope-making, lashing, and sewing — fiber into cordage and cloth.
🎨 Painting, Drawing & Ceramics
Drawing and painting from life, plus clay — pinch-pots to a wheel-thrown bowl.
🌿 Nature & Plants
Seasonal plant ID, leaf prints, tree lore, and which wood is good for what.
🎵 Music & Instruments
Build simple instruments — shakers, drum, panpipes — then play together.
🥾 Hiking, Maps & Navigation
Hikes near and far — making our own maps and finding the way with map and compass.

About

Hands-on, screen-free learning and building for kids and adults, on farms in the Lincoln, MA area. Four ways to take part:

🗓️ ProgramsSign up for a weekly meetup focused on a particular topic. 🔧 WorkshopsSingle-session deep-dives. 🛠️ ProjectsLong-running builds and experiments. 🌲 MeetupsCasual, drop-in gatherings — hikes, plant walks, and goats at the farm.

Waterbear Field School operates as a recreational and enrichment program, not a licensed school or daycare.
