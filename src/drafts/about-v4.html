How it works

Hands-on, screen-free days for kids and their grown-ups, on farms around Lincoln. The day is analog — any electronics stay off to the side — and the meet-up spot is sent to enrolled families each day. There are five ways to take part:

🗓️ ProgramsPick a weekday for the month — a different craft each day. The steady weekly rhythm. 🔧 WorkshopsSingle-session deep-dives — often after-school, kids + a grown-up together. 🛠️ ProjectsLong-running builds & experiments you can follow along with — no sign-up needed. 🌲 MeetupsCasual, drop-in gatherings — hikes, plant walks, and goats at the farm.

Grown-ups welcome to stay or drop off. A recreational program, not a licensed school or daycare.
