Halé and I met up at pasture to talk about ways of weaving WFS curricula into activities that would support Goodwin Hill Goats: helping with feeding and care of the goats, foraging for supplemental goat greens, and helping with infrastructure upkeep and repair. Looking forward to more brainstorming soon!
