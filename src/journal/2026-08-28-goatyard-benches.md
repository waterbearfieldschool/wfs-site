We're working with [Goodwin Hill Goats](/journal/2026-05-23-goodwin-hill-goats/) to help LexFarm get ready for a larger goat herd this fall. One of our first projects was a set of benches that both goats and humans can use.

We wanted the design to be **sturdy and inexpensive**, built from **readily available or scrap lumber** with **simple tools**, and easy for kids and adults with little woodworking experience to put together.

## From precedents to a design

We started by collecting pictures of existing goat benches and picnic-style benches, then narrowed them down to a few favourites. From those we drew up a starter design:

- **Top:** 42″ long, 22″ wide, 17″ high
- **Lumber:** six 2×4s in total, about **$30**
- **The base:** three 2×4s (about $15). Its pieces can be cut, and even pre-assembled, ahead of a workshop.
- **The seat:** once the base is built, the top can be made from whatever boards are on hand: 2×4s, 2×6s, 2×8s or 2×12s.
