Craig and I met up with Ben and Alex of [Edgewood Sailing School](https://edgewoodsailing.org/) to brainstorm about various STEM workshop ideas for their 2026 Sailing School, including an introduction to mesh radio. We're also planning on setting up a water level sensor on the dock, sending its data via mesh radio to the internet. More soon!
